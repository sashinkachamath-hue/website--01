Seeds



Seeds are tiny nutritional powerhouses packed with fiber, plant-based protein, and heart-healthy unsaturated fats. Adding just 1–2 tablespoons daily can help lower cholesterol, stabilize blood sugar, support digestion, and reduce inflammation.

Key Nutritional BenefitsHeart Health.
  1. High in omega : 3 and omega-6 fatty acids that regulate blood pressure and reduce heart disease risk.
  2. Digestive Health: High dietary fiber feeds healthy gut bacteria and prevents constipation.
  3. Mineral Rich: Excellent sources of magnesium, zinc, iron, and calcium.
  4. Antioxidants: Rich in polyphenols and lignans that protect cells from oxidative stress and balance hormones
